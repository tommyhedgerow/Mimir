# 📕 Glossary

> The vault's vocabulary: the niche words the sessions actually used, each with the field it belongs to. One note per term in `Learn/Glossary/`, so every term is a first-class note — **`Cmd-O` finds it, Obsidian's search finds it, and the table below filters it by field.**
>
> A term gets an entry when a session uses it, never before. So the glossary cannot drift from what was actually taught: if a word is here, it turned up in a lesson, and its entry says which layer of it was covered.

## What is here and why

_Nothing yet — and the emptiness is the rule working, not a gap to fill in advance._

Each entry gives the word, a plain definition, the field, and how much of it the teaching actually reached. Terms fall into a handful of fields, named plainly enough to be grouped by, and each session adds to whichever field it touched:

- **Plants & ecology** — the formations and life-history strategies, and the vocabulary of disturbance.
- **Mycology** — the organism and its feeding mode.
- **Botany** — the plant body, and the frames a regional flora is read through.
- **Earth sciences** — the discipline's logic and its arithmetic.
- **Humanities** — the words that let a tradition be read without flattening it.

## How a term gets added

At the write-back of a session, every **niche** word the session leaned on that is not already here gets a note. The bar is deliberately high — terms a general reader would know (drought, nutrient, forest) are not entries; terms that had to be *defined for the lesson to proceed* are. The per-term note is generated from the session's own wording, so a correction recorded in a session propagates into the glossary rather than quietly diverging from it.

**When a session corrects a term, the correction goes into its entry in the same turn and must not be simplified back later.** Getting a term loose is recorded as an error, and the entry is where the tight version is kept — so a reader who meets the loose version elsewhere has somewhere to find out that it is loose.
