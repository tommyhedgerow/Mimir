## 🃏 Cards

_What Anki can hold from this idea, and nothing else. The derivation in *Why it has to be this way* must **not** become a card — a card turns reconstruction into recognition, and the `reviewing` skill is explicit that this is worse than no review. What belongs here is the fact-shaped residue: a name, a date, a character, or the wrong claim this note's *Where I got it wrong* describes. Collected by `node Tools/anki-cards.mjs --all`._

_**A claim to judge, or a question** — `front :: back :: kind` (kind optional: `trap`, `name`, `date`, `species`, `fact`):_

- _The wrong claim, stated as if it were true? — does that hold? :: **No** — what is wrong with it, in one or two sentences._ :: trap

_**Or a cloze card** — one missing token in a true sentence, written `{{c1::…}}`, with its kind required and a caveat optional in the middle (`sentence :: caveat :: kind`):_

- _The Epanagoge of {{c1::886}} broadens the patriarch's powers against Justinian's legislation._ :: date

_Anki makes one card per `{{cN::}}`, so a name and its date in one sentence are two cards — and each gap is visible on the other's card, which is why a gap should hide one thing only._
